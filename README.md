# KawachAR – Industrial AR Safety Training & Verified Certification

KawachAR is a smartphone-oriented industrial safety training platform designed to make safety learning more practical, interactive and verifiable for industrial workers.

The prototype demonstrates an integrated Admin Portal and Worker Mobile App workflow covering facility registration, department management, worker enrollment, AR-based safety training, assessment and digital certification.

## Key Features

- Industrial facility registration
- Active department configuration
- Unique KawachAR Organization ID generation
- Frontline worker enrollment
- Automatic Worker ID generation
- Worker mobile app simulation
- Interactive AR safety training workflow
- Fire & Explosion safety module
- Gas Leak & Confined Space module
- Automatic assessment and scoring
- Pass / Retake mechanism
- Digital safety certificate
- Certificate ID and cryptographic verification
- Admin certificate verification and audit registry
- Mobile-first worker experience

## Safety Training Modules

1. Fire & Explosion Response
2. Gas Leak & Confined Space Protocol
3. Machinery Safety
4. Electrical Hazards
5. PPE & Evacuation

The prototype currently demonstrates the first two modules, while the remaining modules are shown as upcoming modules.

## Purpose

KawachAR aims to transform conventional industrial safety training into an interactive, repeatable and verifiable learning experience using smartphone-based augmented reality concepts.

The platform is designed for industrial environments including mining, manufacturing, steel and related sectors.
